Research conclusion:
- No transparent modern published equation was found whose declared inputs match Tracky's five-segment 20/100 kHz scalar impedance values.
- Contemporary equations require incompatible frequencies, separate resistance/reactance, different electrode paths, or narrowly incompatible calibration populations.
- Matching InBody 230/270 studies validate proprietary outputs without publishing reusable coefficients.

- Relevant native symbols survive in seven ABI builds; x86/x86-64 are the best continuation point.
- The Modern-Apps clean-room calculator is rejected because it ignores most segmental inputs, uses hand-tuned/literature-derived coefficients, and clamps outputs.
